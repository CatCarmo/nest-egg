Welcome to Nest Egg

Here's the quick start:

  1. Set your income and monthly expenses on the Budget tab.
  2. Your emergency fund target is computed from your essentials — it's the orange card just below. Build this first, before investing.
  3. Add other savings goals (phone, trip, big purchase) with + New goal, then go to the Budget tab and assign each a slice of your monthly leftover.
  4. Once a month, click Log this month's savings on the Budget tab to record your progress and watch your history grow.
  5. Use Projections to see what your future savings could grow into in something like the S&P 500 (~10%/year before inflation).

Your data

Back up, restore, or reset. Everything is stored in your browser — exporting is the only way to keep it safe across devices or browser resets.

Install on this device

Add Nest Egg to your home screen as a real app. Once installed, it opens in its own window and keeps working when you're offline.

iOS (Safari): tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android (Chrome/Edge): the install button above turns on when the browser is ready.
Desktop (Chrome/Edge): a small install icon also appears in the address bar.

Export

Download a JSON file with all your goals, expenses, budget, emergency fund, and log history. Keep it as a backup, or use it to move to another browser or device.

Import

Restore from a Nest Egg backup file. This replaces your current data.

Reset

Clear everything and start fresh. This cannot be undone — export a backup first if there's any chance you'll want it back.

Not financial advice. Nest Egg is a personal planning tool. Projections are estimates based on the numbers you type in, they assume a steady rate of return, and they ignore inflation, tax and fees. Real markets do not behave that way. For decisions about your money, talk to a qualified financial adviser.

Your data stays with you. Everything you enter is saved in your own browser on this device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no trackers and no ads. Nothing is ever sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to.
